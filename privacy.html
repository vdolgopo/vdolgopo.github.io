Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

  • What it accesses: with the account owner's permission, Skyscapes Data reads Google Ads reporting data (campaigns, spend, clicks, conversions) for the Skyscapes advertising account. It is read-only and makes no changes.
  • How it is used: only to produce internal reports for Skyscapes. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Storage: report data is kept in a private, access-controlled store used for those reports. Sign-in tokens are stored privately and encrypted where they are kept in the cloud.
  • Removing access: you can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; ask us to delete stored data by emailing the contact below.

Google API data use follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
